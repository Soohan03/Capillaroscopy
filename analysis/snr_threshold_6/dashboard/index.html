How it was done

  • Asked for after unblinding. The PI asked on 6 November 2026 how the results change if loops count from —. The protocol of 29 October fixes — and named its sensitivity analyses before the results were seen. This one is post hoc, and the protocol's results stand.
  • The same lines, the same measurement. No line was placed again or moved. Each loop's pattern shift, and that of its two limbs for the movement check, was computed again from the space-time diagram saved when the loop was measured, and judged at —. The movement check uses the same cutoff, so a loop can also drop out at the lower one.
  • Checked. The same computation at — gives the protocol's tables to float precision, and every run judged the same — loops.
  • Otherwise a loop counts as before: a whole loop over the official rest, on a stabilized video, its speed inside the range searched, its line not crossing itself.

Limits. Few participants: — of — in aim 1 and — of — in aim 2. The cutoff was chosen after the results were known. One observer placed every line.
