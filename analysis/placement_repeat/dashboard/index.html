What it means for aim 1

Choosing and placing the line adds little to the variation. On the same video the observer picked the same reference in — of — recordings, and the same capillary measured twice differed by at most — mm/s. Aim 1's CV between days, —, must then come mostly from what differs between the days: the field of view, which capillaries are in it, and the flow itself.

Where the reference does not count, the value comes from a backup, and which backup is used can change it several-fold. The velocity comparison rests on few pairs, so these figures give the size of the effect, not a precise estimate.

Statistics

As for aim 1: the typical error is the SD of the differences divided by √2, the CV the typical error over the mean of all values, both with 95% intervals from the chi-square distribution; the bias and limits of agreement are the repeat minus the first placement; ICC(A,1) is two-way, absolute agreement, single measurement. Few pairs make the ICC uninformative here.

How it was done

  • Planned before the draw. The protocol of 29 October planned a second blind placement of ten random recordings on another day, under a second observer code. A written plan fixed what it left open (the pool, the seed, the comparisons and their statistics) and was committed and pushed before the recordings were drawn.
  • The draw. Ten of 70 recordings, with numpy's random generator and seed 20261105. The pool was the 72 recordings the aims use, less two: ESMOKE FP03 W after, on which no line stays in the picture over the rest, and ISMOKE FP04 S before, whose lines had been looked at after unblinding.
  • Blind. The app's blind placement listed the recordings under codes, hid every result and fixed the range to the official rest. The repeat had its own data folder holding only the drawn videos, so none of the first placement's lines showed. The observer recognized none of the codes.
  • Placed as the protocol says. Rank the loops of the whole field first, then place up to three whole loops in that order (the reference, then two backups). A loop counts with a signal-to-noise of — or more, its speed inside the range searched, no movement and the official rest.
  • Fixed before the comparison. On one recording a backup had been saved twice on the same capillary, six seconds apart; the copy was removed. No other change was made.

Limits. — recordings, — of them ISMOKE; one observer, one week apart; the velocity comparison rests on — references, — loops with the backups. The repeat sees the same video, so it measures the choice and placement of the line, not the field or the flow, which differ between the days of aim 1.
