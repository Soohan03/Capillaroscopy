# Study metadata

The official times at which the finger was occluded and released in each
nailfold videocapillaroscopy recording of the ESMOKE and ISMOKE studies. They
were exported from DynCap, where the recordings are archived with their
metadata, and added on 2026-10-07. The files are kept exactly as provided.

| File | Study | Recordings | Participants | Conditions |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `ESMOKE_timestamps.csv` | ESMOKE (2019–2020) | 74 | 17 | W = e-cigarette with nicotine, Q = without nicotine |
| `ISMOKE_timestamps.csv` | ISMOKE (2020) | 50 | 13 | S = heated tobacco product, C = no exposure |

## Columns (separated by semicolons, UTF-8)

- `subject`: the participant's code within the study (FP01, FP02, …). The
  same code in ESMOKE and ISMOKE is a different person.
- `occlusion`: seconds from the start of the video when the cuff was inflated
  (200 mmHg at the base of the finger).
- `release`: seconds from the start of the video when it was released. Empty
  where it was not recorded (16 recordings).
- `source_file`: the recording's file name without extension, as the app
  names a recording, built as
  `<subject>_<condition><före|efter>[_försökN]_<date>_<time>`, where före is
  before exposure, efter after, and försök a further attempt. ISMOKE names
  start with `ISMOKE_`.

## In the app (since 2026-10-11)

The Centre line and Two boxes pages offer each recording's phases as ranges,
from these times (`capillary.inventory.recording_phases`):

- **Rest:** from 2 s after the start to 2 s before the occlusion.
- **Occlusion:** from 2 s after the occlusion to 2 s before the release.
- **After release:** from 2 s after the release to 2 s before the video ends.

Every data set saves the official phase of its range (`phase` in its
summary). A recording whose times lie after the end of its video, such as
`FP01_Wefter_14-Oct-2019_10-07-49` above, gets no ranges.

## Which recordings the aims use (`derived/`)

Generated by `python -m capillary.inventory --out metadata/derived` on
2026-10-09; the module's docstring has the rules. The two exports above are
not changed.

- `derived/recordings.csv`: one row per recording, with:
  - the parts of its name and the official times;
  - the rest available (from 2 s after the start to 2 s before the occlusion)
    and whether it is enough (30 s);
  - anything that does not fit;
  - which aim uses it, as what (`used_for`).
- `derived/participants.csv`: one row per participant, with:
  - the visits, the days between them, and which condition came first;
  - the recording chosen for each slot (active and control, före and efter),
    with the other attempts;
  - whether aim 1 and aim 2 can use them: yes, check (an issue to look up in
    DynCap), or no.

When a visit has several attempts of a recording, the last one with enough
rest is chosen. A missing release time does not matter, because both aims
use the rest before the occlusion.

| | ESMOKE | ISMOKE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participants | 17 | 13 |
| Aim 1: the före of both visits | 13, and 1 to check | 9, and 1 to check |
| Aim 2: före and efter of the nicotine visit (W, S) | 12, and 1 to check | 9, and 1 to check |
| Days between the visits (aim 1) | median 20, 6–68 | median 7, 7–16 |
| Active visit first (aim 1) | 7 of 14 | 5 of 10 |

The aims use 72 recordings, 48 of them for aim 1. Until 2026-10-29, when aim 2
was corrected to the nicotine visit alone (`PROTOCOL.md`), they used 90: aim 2
then took the före and efter of both visits.
